ABOUT BIG LAGOON
(EL NIDO)

The Big Lagoon is known for its enchanting emerald waters, which reflect the beauty of the surrounding limestone formations. The calm and serene atmosphere exudes a sense of tranquility, making it the perfect place to unwind and immerse yourself in nature’s wonders.

Whether you’re a snorkeling enthusiast, a kayaking adventurer, or simply want to bask in the splendor of the lagoon, there is something for everyone at the Big Lagoon. Dive into its depths and discover a vibrant underwater world, or paddle through the lagoon’s placid waters and marvel at the stunning karst cliffs that encircle you. It’s a place where dreams come alive and memories are made.

As you journey through the Big Lagoon, you’ll be treated to breathtaking views and encounters with a diverse range of flora and fauna. Keep your eyes peeled for various species of fish, vibrant corals, and even elusive sea turtles that call the lagoon home.

KEY TAKEAWAYS:





-- The Big Lagoon is famous for its stunning emerald-green waters surrounded by towering limestone cliffs.

-- It offers a peaceful and relaxing environment, perfect for nature lovers and travelers seeking tranquility.

-- Popular activities include snorkeling, kayaking, and swimming in its calm, crystal-clear waters.

-- The lagoon is home to diverse marine life such as colorful fish, coral reefs, and sea turtles.

-- Visitors can enjoy breathtaking scenery and unique natural landscapes found in El Nido.

-- It is considered one of the top tourist attractions in the Philippines.

Why Visit Big Lagoon in El Nido?

The Big Lagoon is a must-visit destination known for its stunning emerald waters and towering limestone cliffs, offering one of the most breathtaking views in El Nido. Its calm and peaceful atmosphere makes it an ideal place to relax and enjoy nature, away from the noise of everyday life.

Visitors can also experience a variety of activities such as kayaking, snorkeling, and swimming in its crystal-clear waters. Rich in marine life and surrounded by dramatic scenery, the lagoon offers unforgettable moments for every traveler, making it one of the top attractions in the Philippines.

What to Expect in the Big Lagoon in El Nido Tour





Your adventure begins with a scenic boat ride departing from El Nido town. Local guides share essential safety tips and insider knowledge as you cruise along the stunning coastline.

Approaching Big Lagoon, the dramatic contrast between rugged limestone formations and the serene blue waters creates an unforgettable first impression. Glide into the calm lagoon to experience its tranquil beauty firsthand.

Big Lagoon in El Nido Travel Tips





Make the most of your adventure to the stunning Big Lagoon in El Nido with these essential travel tips:

1. Visit Early or Late: Go in the morning or late afternoon to avoid crowds and enjoy a more peaceful experience in El Nido.

2. Bring Cash: Some fees (like entrance or kayak rental) are paid on-site, so it’s best to have cash ready.

3. Rent a Kayak: Kayaking is one of the best ways to explore the lagoon, especially the shallow inner areas that boats can’t reach.

4. Wear Reef-Safe Sunscreen: Protect your skin while helping preserve the marine life in the Philippines.

5. Use Waterproof Bags: Keep your valuables safe from water while kayaking or swimming.

6. Respect Nature: Avoid touching corals or disturbing marine life to help keep the lagoon beautiful.

7. Book a Tour in Advance: The Big Lagoon is usually part of Tour A in El Nido, so booking early ensures your slot.

Things to do in the Big Lagoon

Visiting the Big Lagoon in El Nido, Palawan offers a multitude of exciting activities and experiences. Here are some of the top things to do in this enchanting destination:

1. Kayaking:

One of the best ways to explore the Big Lagoon is by kayaking. Rent a kayak and paddle through the calm, turquoise waters at your own pace. Discover hidden coves, navigate through limestone formations, and marvel at the rich marine life as you glide through the lagoon’s pristine waters.








2. Swimming:

Take a refreshing dip in the crystal-clear waters of the Big Lagoon. The calm and inviting lagoon is perfect for a leisurely swim, allowing you to cool off while surrounded by stunning natural beauty. Just be mindful of the designated swimming areas and respect the marine ecosystem.








3. Snorkeling:

Put on a mask and snorkel to immerse yourself in the vibrant underwater world of the Big Lagoon. Discover colorful coral reefs teeming with tropical fish, sea turtles, and other marine creatures. Snorkeling here offers a unique opportunity to witness the diverse marine life that calls the lagoon home.








4. Photography:

FThe Big Lagoon is a photographer’s paradise. Capture the breathtaking views and unique rock formations that surround the lagoon. The still waters and towering cliffs provide a stunning backdrop for your photos, creating picture-perfect moments you’ll treasure forever.










5. Beachcombing:

As you explore the shoreline of the Big Lagoon, keep an eye out for seashells and other treasures that may wash up on the beach. Beachcombing can be a relaxing and rewarding activity, allowing you to connect with nature and find small mementos to take home with you.








6. Picnicking:

Bring a packed lunch or grab a beachside spot and enjoy a picnic with a view. The Big Lagoon offers several spots where you can lay out a blanket and indulge in a delicious meal surrounded by stunning natural beauty. Just remember to practice responsible tourism and dispose of any waste properly.










7. Boat Tours:

Many island-hopping tours in El Nido include a stop at the Big Lagoon. Joining a boat tour allows you to relax and enjoy the scenic beauty of the lagoon, as well as visit other nearby attractions. Professional guides will provide insights about the area, making your experience even more enriching.

LOCATION OF BIG LAGOON(EL NIDO):





-- The Big Lagoon is located on the eastern side of Miniloc Island, which is just a short 45-minute boat ride from El Nido Town. To reach the island, you’ll need to hire a boat or go on an island-hopping tour from El Nido.

-- Keep in mind that the boat will not take you directly inside the Big Lagoon. Instead, it will dock near the entrance, and you’ll need to either rent a kayak or swim to get inside. It’s highly recommended that you pay the few bucks for the kayak because the lagoon is massive. Even the most experienced swimmers will have a difficult time exploring everything they want to see in just a few short hours by swimming.

CONCLUSION

In conclusion, the Big Lagoon in El Nido is a breathtaking natural wonder that combines tranquil emerald waters, towering limestone cliffs, and rich marine biodiversity. Whether you choose to kayak, snorkel, swim, or simply relax and take in the scenery, the lagoon offers an unforgettable escape into nature’s beauty. Its serene atmosphere, dramatic landscapes, and abundant wildlife make it a must-visit destination for travelers seeking both adventure and relaxation.

By exploring the lagoon responsibly—respecting marine life, following safety guidelines, and practicing sustainable tourism—visitors can ensure that this iconic destination remains pristine for generations to come. A trip to the Big Lagoon promises memories of stunning vistas, vibrant underwater life, and the peaceful harmony of one of the Philippines’ most celebrated natural attractions.
