ABOUT PALAWAN TRAVEL BLOG

Welcome to our Palawan Travel Blog! Discover the beauty, culture, and unforgettable adventures waiting in Palawan—widely known as one of the most stunning destinations in the Philippines. From pristine beaches and towering limestone cliffs to crystal-clear waters, Palawan is truly a tropical paradise.

Our mission is to inspire and guide travelers as they explore must-visit destinations like El Nido, Coron, and Puerto Princesa. We also aim to provide travelers with clear and helpful information about the destinations they want to visit—from top attractions and activities to travel tips and local insights. Through captivating photos, engaging travel stories, and practical guides, we hope to give you a complete preview of Palawan’s magic—even before your journey begins.
