ABOUT DULI BEACH (EL NIDO)

Duli Beach is one of El Nido’s best-kept secrets a secluded stretch of pristine white sand, turquoise waters, and gently swaying palm trees that seems untouched by time. Unlike the more popular beaches in central El Nido, Duli remains quiet and intimate, making it ideal for travelers seeking an escape from the crowds. Its natural beauty offers more than just a scenic backdrop; it provides a true sense of relaxation and adventure. The beach is surrounded by lush greenery and rugged cliffs that create a dramatic coastline, perfect for photography, contemplation, or simply enjoying nature in its rawest form. Beyond its breathtaking scenery, Duli is also a hub for surfing enthusiasts, where beginners and intermediate surfers can ride gentle to moderate waves.

KEY TAKEAWAYS:





-- Secluded & Peaceful: Escape the hustle of central El Nido and experience a beach that feels like your own private paradise.

-- Surfing Haven: The waves are perfect for beginners and intermediate surfers, with surfboards available to rent nearby.

-- Natural Beauty: Powdery white sand, clear blue waters, and coconut trees make it a stunning backdrop for relaxation and photos.

-- Accessible yet Remote: A 30–40 minute tricycle ride from El Nido town gives you the perfect balance of accessibility and tranquility.

-- Minimal Amenities: Small beachside huts provide basic comfort, but the real luxury is the untouched environment.

What to Expect in the Puerto Princesa Underground River Tour





Visitors to Duli Beach can expect a blend of adventure and relaxation. The beach stretches far enough for long walks, early morning runs, or simply exploring the coastline. The water is clear, making it great for swimming and wading. While the waves are ideal for surfing, they’re also gentle enough for beginners to enjoy with proper guidance. The area is not heavily commercialized, which means you won’t find luxury resorts or crowds but you will find small, locally-run huts, basic food stalls, and friendly locals eager to share their slice of paradise. Nights are serene, perfect for stargazing under an open sky with only the sound of the waves in the background.

Puerto Princesa Underground River Travel Tips





Make the most of your adventure to the stunning Duli Beach in Palawan Island with these essential travel tips:

1. Best Time to Visit: November to May, when the weather is dry and the seas are calmer.

2. What to Bring: Water, snacks, sunscreen, hats, and beach essentials, as amenities are limited.

3. Surfing: Boards can be rented on-site, but booking in advance is recommended during peak season.

4. Local Etiquette: Keep the beach clean, respect the locals, and leave only footprints behind.

5. Getting There: Take a tricycle from El Nido town; the journey itself offers scenic views of rural Palawan.

Activities

While Duli Beach is a quieter, more secluded destination compared to other El Nido spots, it offers a wide range of activities that let visitors enjoy nature and tranquility at their own pace. Here are some highlights to include in your itinerary:

1. Surfing & Bodyboarding:

Catch some waves at Duli Beach, which is perfect for both beginners and experienced surfers. Local surf schools offer lessons and board rentals, making it easy to learn in a safe and friendly environment. The beach’s consistent swell makes it a hidden gem for those looking to ride waves without the crowds.




2. Beachcombing & Sunbathing:

Stroll along the pristine sandy shore, collecting seashells or just feeling the soft sand under your feet. Find a shady spot under coconut trees or set up a hammock to relax while listening to the gentle lapping of the waves. Sunrise and sunset walks here are especially magical, with the sky reflecting on the calm waters.




3. Photography & Nature Walks:

Duli Beach is framed by lush hills and dramatic limestone cliffs, offering countless photo opportunities. Take a nature walk along the nearby trails, explore hidden coves, or capture the breathtaking views of the sea meeting the horizon. Birdwatching enthusiasts will also enjoy spotting native coastal species.




4. Relaxation:

Escape the hustle and bustle with a quiet afternoon in a beachside hut or under a palm tree. Feel the soft sea breeze, enjoy a refreshing coconut or cold drink, and meditate to the sound of the waves. It’s a perfect spot for reading, journaling, or simply soaking in the serenity of untouched nature.

LOCATION OF DULI BEACH (EL NIDO):





-- Duli Beach is located on the northwestern coast of El Nido, along the main road heading toward Nacpan Beach. It is about 30–40 minutes by tricycle or motorcycle from El Nido town proper, making it a quieter and less crowded alternative to the more popular beaches in the area.

-- Duli Beach is known for its long stretch of fine sand, rolling surf, and natural, undeveloped surroundings. It is a popular spot for surfing, swimming, and beach relaxation, and the nearby coastal scenery includes coconut palms, gentle waves, and scenic sunsets that make it a hidden gem for travelers seeking a more peaceful beach experience.
