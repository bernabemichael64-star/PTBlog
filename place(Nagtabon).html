ABOUT NAGTABON
(PUERTO PRINCESA)

DNagtabon Beach is a sprawling, unspoiled coastal haven located just 45 minutes from Puerto Princesa. With its soft golden-white sand, gently rolling waves, and palm-fringed coastline, it’s an ideal destination for families, couples, and solo travelers alike. The beach offers a perfect mix of relaxation and adventure, with areas suitable for swimming, picnics, and beach games. Unlike busy city beaches, Nagtabon maintains its natural charm, with only a handful of small resorts and local eateries scattered along the shore. The surrounding scenery is a combination of lush coconut groves, distant hills, and crystal-clear waters, creating a calm and visually stunning environment.

KEY TAKEAWAYS:





-- Tranquil Escape: Far from the urban bustle, Nagtabon offers peace, space, and natural beauty.

-- Family-Friendly: Calm waves and wide sandy shores make it perfect for children and group activities.

-- Accessible Yet Serene: A short drive from Puerto Princesa allows easy access without sacrificing solitude.

-- Natural Scenery: Palm trees, clean sand, and a gentle surf combine for a relaxing and picturesque experience.

-- Basic Comfort: Small resorts and eateries are available, but the focus is on the natural environment.

Why Visit the Nagtabon Beach (Puerto Princesa)?

Nagtabon Beach is the perfect destination for anyone looking to relax, reconnect with nature, or spend quality time with loved ones. Its wide shores and calm waters provide space for swimming, beach games, or simply basking in the sun. The beach is also ideal for couples seeking a romantic getaway or photographers looking to capture pristine natural landscapes. Whether you’re enjoying a picnic under the palms, taking a leisurely stroll along the shore, or watching a breathtaking sunset, Nagtabon offers an unforgettable experience. It’s a chance to slow down, breathe in the salty sea air, and savor the beauty of Palawan without the distractions of crowded tourist spots.

What to Expect in the Nagtabon Beach (Puerto Princesa) Tour





Visitors can expect a long stretch of soft sand lined with swaying palm trees, gentle waves suitable for swimming and wading, and clear waters that are perfect for relaxing or paddling. The beach is wide and open, providing plenty of space for group activities, sunbathing, and games. Facilities are modest but sufficient, with small resorts, nipa huts, and local eateries offering food and refreshments. The area retains its untouched charm, making it ideal for travelers who want a natural beach experience without heavy commercialization.

Nagtabon Beach Travel Tips





Make the most of your adventure to the stunning Nagtabon Beach in Palawan Island with these essential travel tips:

1. Best Time to Visit : December to May for sunny skies and calm waters.

2. Essentials: Sunscreen, water, snacks, and hats are recommended for a comfortable visit.

3. Respect Nature: Avoid littering and help maintain the beach’s natural beauty.

4. Photography: Early mornings and late afternoons are perfect for capturing stunning light and scenery.

5. Accessibility: Easily reachable by car or motorbike from Puerto Princesa city.

Activities

While Duli Beach is a peaceful and less crowded spot in El Nido, it offers plenty of activities for visitors to enjoy both relaxation and adventure. Swim in calm waters, play beach games, or unwind under coconut trees. Explore scenic trails, capture stunning sunsets, or snorkel among colorful marine life—there’s something for every traveler to enjoy at their own pace.

1. Swimming & Wading:

The gentle waves and clear waters of Duli Beach make it perfect for both adults and children. Take a refreshing dip, float along the calm shoreline, or simply splash in the shallow waters while enjoying the tropical sun.





2. Beach Sports:

The wide sandy shore is ideal for volleyball, frisbee, or other group games. Whether you’re with friends, family, or fellow travelers, the soft sand provides plenty of space for fun and energetic activities.





3. Picnicking & Relaxation:

Relax under the shade of coconut trees or set up a picnic by the shore. Savor fresh snacks, cold drinks, or a packed meal while listening to the soothing sounds of the waves. It’s also a perfect spot to unwind with a book or just daydream.





4. Photography & Nature Walks:

Duli Beach is framed by dramatic cliffs, lush greenery, and pristine sands—perfect for photography enthusiasts. Capture stunning sunsets, unique rock formations, or playful waves. Short nature walks along the coastline reveal hidden nooks and offer panoramic views of the surrounding landscapes.





5. Short Hikes & Exploration:

Venture a little inland to discover small trails and lookout points. These easy hikes provide breathtaking vistas of the beach, the turquoise sea, and the nearby hills. It’s a great way to combine light adventure with scenic observation.

LOCATION OF NAGTABON(PUERTO PRINCESA):





-- Nagtabon Beach is located in the western part of Puerto Princesa City, along the coastline of the Sulu Sea. It is about 20–25 minutes by tricycle or car from the city center, making it easily accessible while still feeling remote and peaceful.

-- Nagtabon Beach is known for its long stretches of golden sand, clear waters, and rolling waves, making it a favorite spot for surfing, swimming, and beach picnics. Surrounded by natural scenery and coconut trees, it offers a more tranquil alternative to the busier city beaches, providing a relaxing escape for both locals and tourists seeking a serene coastal experience.

CONCLUSION

Nagtabon Beach is more than a day trip, it’s an experience of pure relaxation and natural beauty. Its tranquil waters, golden sands, and peaceful surroundings make it an ideal escape from city life. Whether visiting for family fun, romantic moments, or a personal retreat, Nagtabon Beach leaves visitors refreshed, inspired, and deeply connected to the serene magic of Palawan.
