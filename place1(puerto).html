ABOUT PUERTO PRINCESA SUBTERRANEANRIVER NATIONAL PARK

Puerto-Princesa Subterranean River National Park encompasses one of the world’s most impressive cave systems, featuring spectacular limestone karst landscapes, pristine natural beauty, and intact old-growth forests and distinctive wildlife. It is located in the south-western part of the Philippine Archipelago on the mid western coast of Palawan, approximately 76 km northwest of Puerto Princesa and 360 km southwest of Manila.

The property, comprising an area of approximately 22,202 ha, contains an 8.2km long underground river. The highlight of this subterranean river system is that it flows directly into the sea, with its brackish lower half subjected to tidal influence, distinguishing it as a significant natural global phenomenon. The river’s cavern presents remarkable, eye catching rock formations. The property contains a full mountain-to-sea e cosystem which provides significant habitat for biodiversity conservation and protects the most intact and noteworthy forests within the Palawan biogeographic province. Holding the distinction of being the first national park devolved and successfully managed by a local government unit, the park’s effective management system is a symbol of commitment by the Filipino people to the protection and conservation of their natural heritage.

Why Visit the Puerto Princesa Underground River?

The Puerto Princesa Underground River is one of the best places to visit in Puerto Princesa City and a must-experience highlight of any trip to Palawan Island. Stretching over 8 kilometers, it holds the distinction of being the world's longest underground river accessible to travelers through guided boat tours.

Prepare to be mesmerized by towering chambers, intricate stalactites, and unique rock formations resembling animals, fruits, and even religious figures. The river is also home to diverse wildlife, including colonies of bats, swiftlets, and freshwater crabs. As one of the must-visit attractions on Palawan Island, the Underground River offers a unique adventure you won't forget.

What to Expect in the Puerto Princesa Underground River Tour





The Puerto Princesa Subterranean River activity is one of the best and must-try tours on Palawan Island, offering a captivating journey through a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Once you arrive in Sabang Port, settled everything at at the Tourism Office, boarded the boat to the Underground River's entrance, you'll transfer to a smaller paddle boat and start the cave tour.

The journey inside the Puerto Princesa Underground River is like stepping into a natural cathedral. Expect to be mesmerized by towering chambers and impressive rock formations carved by nature over millions of years. Look out for stalactites and stalagmites that resemble shapes such as animals, fruits, and even religious figures

As your guide's searchlight reveals the cave's hidden wonders, you'll hear the echoing calls of bats and see swiftlets flitting across the dark ceiling. The cool air inside the cave provides a refreshing escape from the tropical heat outside. Be sure to look out for unique features such as the iconic "Cathedral" chamber and rock formations with whimsical shapes that spark the imagination.

Puerto Princesa Underground River Travel Tips





Make the most of your adventure to the stunning Puerto Princesa Underground River in Palawan Island with these essential travel tips:

1. Dress Comfortably: Wear lightweight, breathable clothing that you don’t mind getting wet. It's also a good idea to bring a change of clothes.

2. Choose the Right Footwear: Aqua shoes, trekking sandals, or rubber slippers provide better grip and comfort during the tour.

3. Secure Your Belongings: Pack a dry bag to protect your valuables from water splashes.

4. Sun and Bug Protection: Apply sunscreen and mosquito repellent before your tour to stay protected and comfortable.

5. Prioritize Safety: Always wear the life vest and hard hat provided for the tour.

6. Go Plastic-Free: Opt for refillable water bottles instead of disposable ones to help preserve the environment.

7. Be Mindful Inside the Cave: Keep your mouth closed when looking up—you wouldn’t want an unexpected "gift" from the bats above.

8. Respect Cave Rules: Maintain silence inside the chambers to avoid disturbing the wildlife and enhance the serene atmosphere.

9. Choose a central location: To make your trip to the Underground River more convenient, consider staying at hotels in Puerto Princesa City center or Sabang town. These locations provide easier access to the river, reducing travel time.

Nearby Attractions and Activities

While the Underground River tour is a must-see, there are plenty of other incredible attractions and activities in Puerto Princesa City that will make your visit even more unforgettable. Here are some highlights to add to your itinerary:

1. Sabang X-Zipline

For an adrenaline rush with stunning views, don’t miss the Sabang X-Zipline. The adventure begins with a scenic 30-minute trek through the mangroves, leading you up a small mountain to the zipline launch point.

Suspended 150 feet above sea level and stretching 800 meters long, the zipline lets you soar through the air while taking in breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains, river park, and open sea. It’s an exhilarating experience that pairs well with the natural beauty of the area.

2. Ugong Rock Adventures

Ugong Rock is one of the top adventure tours in the Philippines and makes a perfect complement to the Underground River tour. This stunning limestone formation rises 75 feet high and offers a thrilling mix of activities for all types of adventurers.

Ugong Rock Adventures features ziplining with a heart-racing 71-meter drop, along with trekking, rock climbing, zip biking, and base jumping. For those seeking even more excitement, spelunking is available, where you can navigate through narrow passages and explore underground caverns.

The adventure culminates in a breathtaking 360-degree view from the top, making this a must-try experience for adrenaline junkies!

3. Monkey Trail

For a more immersive experience, consider hiking back to Sabang via the 5-kilometer Monkey Trail (also known as the Jungle Trail) after your Underground River tour. This scenic trail takes you through lush forests, towering limestone cliffs, and diverse wildlife.

Along the way, you may encounter playful long-tailed macaques and, with a bit of luck, the rare endemic monitor lizards. The steep climb and off-the-beaten-path route make this a thrilling option for adventure seekers looking to fully immerse themselves in the natural beauty of the area.

4. Sabang Mangrove Paddle Boat Tour

For a peaceful yet thrilling side trip to your Underground River tour, consider the Sabang Mangrove Paddle Boat Tour. As you glide through the tranquil waters, you'll be surrounded by lush mangrove forests that play a key role in preserving the unique ecosystem of Palawan Island.

This top eco-tourism experience not only offers a chance to spot various species of birds and marine life, but also empowers the local community by supporting sustainable practices that protect these vital ecosystems.

LOCATION OF PUERTO PRINCESA SUBTERRANEAN RIVER:





-- Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park is located in the Philippines on the western island of Palawan. It is in the northern part of the island and covers an area of 54,860 acres (22,202 ha).

-- The park is found within the St. Paul Mountain Range about 50 miles (80 km) north from the city Puerto Princesa. The subterranean river has a second floor which means there are waterfalls inside the cave. The second floor features a 980 feet (300 m) cave with bats.

-- Limestone karst mountains are the dominant terrain above ground. St. Pauls Underground Cave is more than 15 miles (24 km) long with 5.1 miles (8.2 km) of the Cabayugan River being underground. It is the second-largest underground river in the world.

-- The cave features stalactites, stalagmites, and several large chambers as part of the underground adventure.

-- Above ground, the national park is home to rich biodiversity with eight of the thirteen types of forests present. Forest types include forest over ultramafic soils, forest over limestone soils, freshwater swamp forest, lowland evergreen, montane, riverine, beach forest, and mangrove. There are over 800 plant species that make this garden paradise for those interested in different types of flora.

-- There are only 30 species of mammals and 19 species of reptiles. The long-tailed macaque monkey is the most frequently observed mammalian species. This is more of a place to experience the wildlife of the skies with 252 species of birds.

-- Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site as well as a Ramsar Wetland Site of importance. It is one of the more popular national parks in the Philippines.
