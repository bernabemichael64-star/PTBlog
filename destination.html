ABOUT THE 12 BEAUTIFUL PLACES IN PALAWAN

Palawan is home to many breathtaking destinations that attract travelers from around the world. Some of its most famous spots include the Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site with a long underground river, and the stunning Big Lagoon and Small Lagoon, known for their clear waters and limestone cliffs. In Coron, visitors can explore the historic Coron Bay Wrecks and swim in the crystal-clear Kayangan Lake, while beach lovers can relax at Nacpan Beach. Hidden gems like Hidden Beach and the rich marine life of Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park offer unique adventures. Visitors can also enjoy island hopping in Honda Bay, explore the historic Tabon Caves, or unwind at peaceful beaches like Duli Beach and Nagtabon Beach. Together, these places showcase the natural beauty, adventure, and rich history that make Palawan one of the best travel destinations in the Philippines.
